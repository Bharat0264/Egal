Responsive screenshots and complete booking tests were repeated against the updated production model. The environment is materially cleaner and remains stylised; this is not a claim that the whole real-time world now matches a photographic CGI render.

## Architecture

- One lazy R3F world and one native scroll container; camera and hero pose derive from the same road curve. Scene windows gate both Three groups and DOM panels. GSAP manages the overlay, not a second scroll controller.
- SelectionContext holds category, body colours, service and booking state. Shared pure validation is imported by browser and Node server. Prices are recomputed from the car data on the server.
- Five static HTML routes are built with React server rendering plus a small prerender script. The 3D scene loads after START, skip, booking intent or START hover/focus. `/simple` requests no WebGL assets.
- The hero GLB is compressed, grounded and batched within its named rig. Repeated world props use instancing; expensive world shadow maps and postprocessing are absent. Procedural reflection lighting, contact shadows and reduced-detail mobile materials remain.

The studio contains 104 objects in Blender data: five cameras, five lights, two steering empties and 92 meshes including hidden studio/support content. Production-visible meshes: **79**, **59,102 triangles**, **12 materials**. All four wheel minima are exactly z=0. Body and Cabin_Glass topology inspection reported no non-manifold edges, boundary edges or loose vertices. The body uses BodyPaint and Trim; only its painted faces use BodyPaint. Front wheels retain steering parents. BodyPaint has metallic ~0.78, roughness ~0.22 and coat weight 1; Tire roughness ~0.9, Rim metallic ~0.9. See the full material inventory rather than assuming the source has only the original seven materials.

Used the supported `look` tool for rear three-quarter, side and front material previews, and rendered the existing studio camera to `assets/hardening-checks/blender-preview.png` at 800×534 / 12 Cycles samples. Temporary render settings were restored. The render confirms a complete generic coupe/crossover car with shaped panels, wheels, glazing, lamp assemblies and trim; it is not a single-box placeholder. Its silhouette is lower and more coupe-like than the original tall-SUV reference. No design change, model replacement, new export or fake source edits are claimed in this pass. The recoverable source and existing optimized GLB remain intact.
